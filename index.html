How CityGuard Works

1Detect

A service goes offline — manually, from a scenario, or from the automated demo. CityGuard registers it as the origin of an incident.

2Propagate

The engine walks the dependency graph wave by wave. Losing one provider degrades a service; losing enough of them fails it.

3Analyze

Every metric — cascade depth, affected services, critical risk, resilience score — is computed from the live graph state, never hard-coded.

4Recover

Restoration is progressive. As providers come back, dependents heal automatically and the resilience score climbs back to 100.
