About This Simulation

CityGuard models urban infrastructure as an interconnected dependency graph and simulates how disruptions propagate across services, helping operators understand cascade impact and recovery.

Built for the S-03: Urban Infrastructure Cascade Simulator challenge — representing service dependencies as a dynamic graph, simulating failure and recovery over simulated time, and measuring cascade depth, affected services, and recovery duration in real time.
